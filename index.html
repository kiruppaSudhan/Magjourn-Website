Who we are

Where healthcare meets intelligent engineering.

MAGJOURN is an innovation-driven team focused on developing intelligent healthcare technologies through the integration of artificial intelligence, medical imaging, and engineering.

We aim to transform healthcare challenges into practical, technology-driven solutions that support better diagnostics and patient care.

Our flagship project, MAGJOURN, explores AI-assisted electromagnetic capsule endoscopy to advance gastrointestinal imaging through intelligent image analysis and magnetically assisted navigation. At MAGJOURN, we believe in interdisciplinary collaboration, research-driven innovation, and building technologies that address real-world healthcare needs.

Project description

How the research process works

The capsule captures visual data from the gastrointestinal tract. Those images provide input for AI-assisted analysis, while electromagnetic actuation and intelligent navigation are explored as research pathways for controlled positioning.
